Bias in AI

AI bias happens when an AI system produces unfair, unbalanced, or inconsistent outcomes. Bias does not always mean the system was designed to be unfair — it can appear on its own.

Important Clarification

Different outcomes are not always bias

A fair decision can differ when the relevant facts are different. The question is what caused the difference.

Relevant Difference Reasonable
Applicant A has a steady income.
Applicant B has no income yet.
→ Different outcomes may be reasonable.
Irrelevant Difference Possible Bias
Both applicants have the same income and payment history.
Only an unrelated detail (like their email) changes.
→ Different outcomes may indicate bias.

Key Takeaways

  • • AI bias can create unfair or inconsistent outcomes.
  • • Bias may come from data, design, prompts, or usage.
  • • Similar cases should be compared carefully.
  • • Only relevant information should influence a decision.
  • • Human review and continuous testing are important.
  • • Bias can be reduced, but not assumed to be fully removed.

Summary

AI bias happens when irrelevant patterns or assumptions influence an outcome. Testing similar cases helps reveal whether an AI system is behaving consistently and fairly.
