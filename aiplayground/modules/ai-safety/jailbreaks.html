Jailbreaks

A jailbreak tries to make an AI bypass its safety rules by hiding the real intent of a request inside a story, a role, or clever wording. Safety systems aim to reduce harm while still allowing normal use.

Important Distinction

Jailbreaks vs prompt injection

Jailbreaks
Target: the model's safety rules
Source: user behaviour
Goal: bypass restrictions
Result: unsafe model output
Prompt Injection
Target: the application's instructions
Source: user or external content
Goal: change system behaviour
Result: altered workflow or data misuse
Jailbreaks focus on the model. Prompt injection focuses on the system around it. They can overlap.

Key Takeaways

  • • Jailbreaks try to bypass a model's safety rules.
  • • Intent matters more than wording. Reframing does not change the goal.
  • • Jailbreaks target the model; prompt injection targets the system around it.
  • • Layered safety beats any single rule, and safe redirection keeps it helpful.
  • • Detection is a balance: too strict blocks normal use, and no system is perfectly safe.

Summary

Jailbreaks are attempts to bypass AI safety rules by changing wording, context, or framing. Safety systems use multiple layers to reduce risk while still allowing normal and helpful use.
