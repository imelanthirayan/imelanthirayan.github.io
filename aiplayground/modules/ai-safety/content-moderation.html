Content Moderation

Content moderation checks whether content is appropriate and safe before it is accepted, generated, displayed, or acted on. Moderation can happen before the AI responds and after the AI creates a response.

Key Takeaways

  • • Content moderation checks both input and output.
  • • Moderation may allow, block, rewrite, review, or escalate content.
  • • Context and intent matter.
  • • A safe input does not guarantee a safe output.
  • • Automated moderation is useful but not perfect.
  • • Human review helps with unclear cases.
  • • Content moderation is one layer of AI safety.

Summary

Content moderation helps AI applications decide what content should be allowed, blocked, changed, or reviewed. Good moderation considers both the content itself and the context in which it is being used.
