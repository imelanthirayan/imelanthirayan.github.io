What is AI Safety?

AI Safety is the practice of making AI systems reliable, trustworthy, and safe for people to use. Before an AI system is trusted in real applications, its answers need to be checked.

Why It Matters

Safety checks sit between AI and the user

AI systems can make mistakes, misunderstand instructions, or produce harmful content if nothing checks their output. Safety checks catch these problems before the answer reaches people.

Key Takeaways

  • • AI Safety helps AI systems become more reliable.
  • • AI can still make mistakes.
  • • Different applications need different safety checks.
  • • Safety combines evaluation, protection, and human oversight.
  • • AI Safety is an ongoing process, not a one-time task.
