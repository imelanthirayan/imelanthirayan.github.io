Evaluating AI Responses

Evaluation is the process of checking whether an AI response is good enough for its intended use. A response can sound clear and confident while still being incomplete, unsupported, or incorrect.

Key Takeaways

  • • AI responses should be checked before they are trusted.
  • • A good response is relevant, accurate, complete, clear, supported, and safe.
  • • Confident wording does not prove correctness.
  • • Evaluation depends on the purpose and risk of the task.
  • • High-impact responses require stronger checks.
  • • Human review remains important.

Summary

Evaluating an AI response means checking whether it is relevant, accurate, complete, clear, supported, and safe for the task. A response should be trusted because it passes these checks, not because it sounds confident.
