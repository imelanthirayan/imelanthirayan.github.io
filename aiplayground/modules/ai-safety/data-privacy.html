Data Privacy

Data privacy means protecting personal, confidential, and sensitive information when it is collected, shared, processed, stored, or generated by an AI system. A good rule: an AI application should use only the information the task needs.

Key Takeaways

  • • AI systems should receive only the data they need.
  • • Sensitive fields can be removed, masked, or replaced, and outputs should be checked for private information.
  • • Tool access should be limited so data does not leave without a clear need.
  • • Privacy and security are related but different.
  • • Masking reduces risk but does not guarantee anonymity, so important decisions may need human review.

Summary

Data privacy in AI means using only the information the task needs, protecting sensitive fields, limiting access, and checking that private information does not appear in the final response.
